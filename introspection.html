Introspection

No Creation of Youtube videos. I can watch the occational youtube video but I should not be "blogging" my life on there because to a certain degree its attention seeking. Also by creating these videos I'm not growing myself in anyway, I'm only further fucking up my mental health by making it so that everything I do has to be "productive". For example, rather than just enjoying a video game own my own, I feel the need to share it with others too which is cringe and fuck and questions whether I even like the video game of if I simply enjoy the thought of others watching me afterwards and doing something "productive". It also increases my baselines dopamine. In a pinch, if I'm focused on improving my life, then there's no point in recording my progression for others.

Another reason I believe that I wanted to create youtube video's is because I wanted others to see my face and acknowledge me. This is a stupid motive because no amount of views will ever remedy this need of acknowledgement, which is rooted is a lack of self acceptance/value. I'm essentially looking for other people to accept me because I can't accept myself. This is unhealthy because it leads to the typical "it's never enough" scenario. Happiness doesn't require magnitude, it requires perspective.

I've noticed that blocking the suns light from my room and instead just having my ceiling light (which is tinted yellow/orange) on creates a much more cozy environment for me and reduces my restlessness.

Before you do anything, think about how it'll benefit you/what you want to get out of doing it -- it's not always worth it.

So I sit here at my desk on a beutiful warn and sunny summer afternoon with nothing to do. I don't know what to do with myself, so I've resorted to writting in here again. All throughout my semsters I dreamed of how blissfully I'd enjoy my free time during the summer, but here I am ruminating in my room doing nothing. Why don't I feel like gaming? Why don't I feel like doing some yard work? Why don't I feel like even watching a movie? Here what I think: My mind is too relaxed and free at the moment to yearn for these distractions, or as some may refer to them, "copes". During my semesters these sorts of activities would bring me pleasure becuase they help me escape from my fixed and tiresome daily routine of studying. So in other words, putting myself in a position of suffering allowed me to experience pleasure through something which causes relatively less suffering to me (i.e. gaming, movies, etc). By this logic two things are manifested. 1. That something can only be "good" (feel good) if it's more stimulating relative to you're baseline stimulation level. Notice how constatly searching to pleasure yourself is unsustainable as the amount you'd need to be stimulated to acheive pleasure would snowball to unattainable levels, inevitably leading to a crash or you searching for extreme ways to fulfill even your baseline amount of stimulation. 2. How all of human emotion can be condensed into the duality of suffering and stimulation. So now knowing this, we can easly see that sustainable "happiness" (I put that word in quotes because all it really is is a steady perculation of stimulation that feels very balanced and healthy) cannot be achived through actively seeking stimulation because of the snowball phenomina which inevitably leads to self distructive behavior that we discussed earlier. In fact, if you really want to be happy, I think you should be avoiding as much stimulation as possible. But obviously thats not realistic. The only realistic way I can see "happiness" actually being achieved is through actively wanting to do things which make you suffer, being content with it, and then on the side experiencing a static and low frequency stream of stimulative activities. (e.g. gaming only twice a week whilst working a full-time job you loathe). And to further add to this - i'm pulling thi statistic out of my ass but it seems reasonable intuitively -- I think it's impossible to spend more than even 25% of our lives happy because of how easily our baseline stimulation levels can increase after a stimulating experience. Hoesntly, it's probably even lower that 25%. Now this makes me ponder whether we should even be striving for happiness if only a small fracation of our lives can be spent experiencing it. Should we be striving for contentment, or something akin to nirvana? I don't know. Should we not be ambicious? Is ambition the killer of joy?

On hatred and anger. It's something thats perpetually eating away at my serenity. Now this entry isn't going to be on why I experience these moments of intense hatred (although that would be an interesting entry) or why I succumb to them when I'm perfectly familiar with stoicism and Buddhism. Rather, it will be about how entertaining this hatred affects me emotionally, and how I can isolate this emotion from the anger it entails to become immune to it’s typical destructive nature. So, the crux of this discussion is how can we become elusive to the negative consequences of hatred while being immersed in it. (The negative consequences being, anger, hyperfixation, and doomerism). Note that this discussion hinges on the distinction between hatred and anger. The first step is becoming familiar with and understanding hatred both in theory and practice. I already feel perfectly acquainted with hatred so I won’t explain it here and obviously there are many other sources which would explain it better. I garner a deep hatred for almost everything in this world, but even still, I won’t specify because I want this analysis to be as general as possible. I‘ve just realized that hyperfixation and doomerism simply branch off anger, so we can prune the negative consequences of hatred down to just anger. My goal is to make it so my actions which are fueled by hatred don’t cause me anger but rather fulfillment. This may seem easy and even intuitive but hatred can morph into anger extremely easily. I’ve sat on this sentence for quite some time now, and I think the solution is to remember the difference between hatred and anger. When I’m doing something which is fueled by hatred, I shouldn’t on the edge of my seat anticipatedly awaiting the recipient’s response, I should be content with the orb of hatred I’ve cursed that person with. I should feel fulfillment and contentment. From the opposite point of view, when someone is hateful towards me, I should interpret it as an indicator of the person already being perpetrated by hatred, and thus my work has already been done. To me, seeing an individual act hatefully towards me should be seen as a good omen of the world being that much more hateful.

On the use of AI. Today I was antsy to tackle a project I had long planned. I wanted to create my own Fortnite skin checker bot. A while back I had decided to look up some open source ones to see what kind of challenge I'd be up to. Alas upon finding a repo, I realized that this was no simple 1 day project (at least for me it wouldn't be). At the time I concluded that if I wanted to make my own I'd have to read and understand this repo line by line. I decided to do it some other time. Today was that other time. I was going to start the dreaded process of understanding the source code line by line when I thought, maybe AI could help me out with this. After all, I've heard of soo many fabulous stories of people using AI to create incredibly successful apps. In hindsight, these was mostly just ads that got in my head JFL. Anyways, I went along with using AI for this ski checker, at first it seemed to give me good, clean, commented source code. But when I tried to run this this web app using a nodejs server, I kept running into the same error I was unaware of how to solve, AI was also helpless of resolving this. When I brought the issue up to it and even gave error codes, it kept recessing the code to earlier outlines of the source code which would obviously not work. You may be asking, well why dont you just debug the damn thing with the console. Here's the issue, I was so far lost on what the code was supposed to do that I didn't even know where to begin. Sure I could've backtracked and understood the code line by line, but at that point it would've been more efficient to just go with my initial plan of analyzing the source code from that one repo I found. I was very frustrated at myself for expecting code which I didn't even fully understand to work. I wasn't leaning anything by doing this project which ultimately was my goal in tackling it. In my personal projects I dont see a single use case where AI could actually enrich me, all it does is deprive. So I wrote this all to convince myself beyond a shadow of a doubt that I should never be utilizing AI in endeavours which I expect to learn from (that being literally all of them apart from work/school).

Today I also had to reaffirm that learning, learning, and learning is imperative to my happiness. After the AI incident I was a bit lost but now I've concluded that the way I utilized AI was actually counter active to my learning. So anyways, I do want to keep learning, but something has become evident to me with regards to how I go about learning. It seems to me that I entertain the notion of learning about things I should learn more than I actually learn about things. It's a strange complex I have and I'll try to unpack it in the next few sentences. Why am I quick to learn about things I should learn? I am afraid that I won't be able to learn everything that I want to learn; I'm afraid that I'll miss out on some vital chunk of knowledge that I would've benefited from greatly. This is a self destructive outlook to have. Firstly because it's flawed in that I'll never be able to learn everything that would enrich me. There is simply too much knowledge. I would even claim that knowledge can grow infinitely. Secondly because the more I learn about the things I want to learn, the more i realize how little I know, and the seemingly insurmountable amount of learning I need to do before I get to where I want to he (knowledge wise). It's a discouragement because I keep throwing books before the door of death, and it grows into a pile I realize I'll never be able to get through. This is opposed to naturally stumbling upon books as I inch my way towards the door of death. I think there is an aspect of doing more and thinking about what I'm going to do less in this introspection. I know that's something I struggle with. So I hope this entry helps me remember that over planning is a bad thing, and sometimes you just need to become immersed into what feels right and not think about where it fits in on enriching you, because that leads to overthinking things and learning about more thing you need to learn. And learning about things you should learn, is something that you should encounter naturally and not seek out.
