Crux: Escape/Re-interpret our flesh-chambers while retaining our conciousness.
Escape where to?: To a reality where my conciousness imagines things being the way I want them to be.

Approach 1. Recognise that my conciousness holds tightly onto my initial interpretations of my senses, the interpretations that I've continously affirmed since birth. Desire vs Expectation

I got into a discussion with a very insightful individual on discord about goals, expectations, and disappointment. Here was the takeaway: having expectations is unnecessary, it puts you under pressure and can lead to disappointment in many cases. What then; are we just supposed to give up on all our goals to relieve ourselves of these pressures? Is that tradeoff even worth it? Turns out there doesn’t need to be tradeoff, there is a way to retain our goals without expectation. Note that goals at their core are a bundle of desire and expectation. We need to retain the former while letting go of the latter. It is once we do this that having goals won’t entail feelings of inadequacy when we fail. Admittedly, letting go of expectations is not something that I’ve fully grasped as of yet, I will allow it some time to sink in.

On optimism in relation to stimulation (refactoring a previous outlook)

I am scared of my ADHD brain becoming trapped in a spiraling cycle of demanding more and more stimulation. This was a concern to me since the age of 16. As such, as you’ve probably (not) already read in “about me” I starved myself of stimulation. I don’t regret this venture; however, I do regret making the mistake of associating optimism as yet another way my brain receives unnecessary stimulation. In retrospect, optimism should’ve been the single channel I allowed to stimulate my brain. Optimism isn’t like video games or other materialistic stimulants; it’s a natural and can’t be taken away from you – assuming you’re level-headed enough. This makes it very powerful as it can be used enhance your eagerness to carry out your goals. Optimism is the one vital source of stimulation that should never be extinguished. I’m not saying that I should stop hating myself, I’m saying that I should reframe my self hate in an optimistic way. Optimism is beautiful.

I already feel perfectly acquainted with hatred so I won’t explain it here and obviously there are many other sources which would explain it better.
