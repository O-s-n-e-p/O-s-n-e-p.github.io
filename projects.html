Replacing Left Click Switch Razer Deathadder

Depicted: Fixed Razer mouse top left; donor mouse on the right. Used up switch in between the two. Solder iron at the bottom.

After 5 years of gaming my razer mouse's left click switch finally shit itself. Now whenever I try to use it, it either doesn't click at all or it double clicks. I was gonna order a new one when I remembered that I've got a soldering iron in my closet. I'm writting this out a week after I already did this repair, and I wasn't planning on documenting the repair on my website a week ago, so I don't have any photos of the procedure. (i'm also too lazy to reopen the mouse to get the proper photos).
This is how it went: Unscrewed the 3 star shaped screws on the underside, one of them being underneith a sticker with voids the warranty. removed the top cover off. Further, unscrewed the PCB from the bottom of the mouse. Heated up the soldering iron and tapped the 3 pins holding the switch in place trying to melt the solder and get it free. Success. I do the same thing with a spare junk mouse to get the replacement switch. Solder that one in and voila.
