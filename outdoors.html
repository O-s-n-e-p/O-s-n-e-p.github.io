Outdoors

Contrary to what you may believe I actually do enjoy being surrounded by nature a lot. What I don't like is when there are other people around me trying to enjoy the same nature I'm trying to enjoy. For some reason, my inhibition is so strong that I can't ever feel fully relaxed unless there is absolutely nobody around me. Walking outside alongside other people, or even just seeing the occational passerby, is not relaxing at all for me. It's not complete torture, but it's not relaxing either, which is the entire point of me even being out in nature.

I don't have even close to enough saved up to buy my dream rural home, but hopefully one day I'll get there.
