Small Motors

Preface: It's not just small motors I take a liking to, I like big ones too. The thing is, I don't have the resources to fully dabble with things like cars. What I mean by that is, I don't have a hoist nor a flat driveway where I can safely do repairs to the underside of the car, or replace transmissions etc. Because of that restraint I've decided not to even bother turning to cars as a hobby, there is simply just too many things I'm unable to do without a hoist. And I don't like the idea of having to take my car to the shop and get someone else to handle my machine. Thats why I compromised with gadgetry that uses small motors. This is perfect for me, because literally every part is accessable without a hoist and so everything that would need to be done can be done by me at home. This is also not to mention that these are cheaper to buy and maintain.

Osnep's Garage

At the moment, the garage is empty. I do have experience though. Last year I found a thrown out weed wacker. It was in seemingly unsalvagable condition; rust all over, old gas still in the tank, missing fuel lines, a busted carborator. Even still I took it home and got to fixing it. I ended up rebuilding the carborator with a carborator repair kit I got from aliexpress. I also replaced the fuel lines and cleaned the tank. I even changed the throttle cable becuase that was busted too lol. Once I did all that, it looked ready to go but it wouldn't start after what should've been a sufficient number of cranks. Turned out that the exhaust was stuffed chalk full of carbon too, so air wasn't even being let through properly... I tried clearning it out, but it was so rusted I wasn't able to fit it back in. At this point I couldn't be bothered to get a exhaust replacement. So I just removed the exhaust entirely. When I started it, it worked, but it was loud as fuck. I didn't have any use for it so I just left it in the garage. When my father came back from vacation he thought the thing was junk and thew it out. I wasn't that upset because it didn't even have trimmer head.
