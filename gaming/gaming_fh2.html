8 year old me obsessed over this game. Every time my mother gave me gaming time, my mind wouldn't even have to ponder which game I'd choose. I loved the game so much that I begged my mother for every single DLC and expansion (she delivered). I even replayed the campaign of the game multiple times. FH2 will forever be my favorite racing game.

Of course it's not the best on paper, but the nostaglia is just too blinding. Whenever I was at my fathers place (no xbox) and I had PC access, I'd always watch Forza Horizon 2 youtube videos. There were 2 Forza youtubers that I enjoyed watching the most, AR12 Gaming and SLAPTrain, both of which were Canadians.
