The original Fortnite experience. Given BR's popularity, it wouldn't suprise me to find out that most people today belive Fortnite: Battle Royale was the original gamemode. In truth, it wasn't. Fortnite was initially supposed to be limited to STW. For some time short time it was, but them Fortnite released BR and seemed to completely abandon development of STW after seeing how profitable the former was. It's really saddening because of how much effort went into developing STW, and just as it was almost done (it only needed some polishing touches; an actual storyline for Twine Peaks; and something like Ventures which added an end game) they abandoned development. In doing so they also robbed founders who payed to play the game in beta before its alledged f2p release. Founders thought that their money would go towards the development of STW, but it didn't.

What's sadder is, not only has serious development of the game been abandoned, but the few updates which do come out only make the game worse and worse. And it's not my subjective opinion deeming these updates bad; they objectively are bad. Every update without fail, for every bug that's fixed 2 more crop up. Some time ago they also changed the graphics, ridding the game of it's dim post-apocalypic atmophere. They also for some reason decided it would be a good idea to mix assets of different art style - go figure. I mean the list goes on and on; but with the recent F2P update, they seemed to not even bother trying to cover up how their ruining the game on purpose. I won't get into it, as others online have covered it in more detail than I'd bother to do here. This now leads to my not-so-far-fetched conspiracy that Epic's ultimate plan for STW is to shut down the servers. Their excuse for doing so would be it's low player count; hence making the game progressively more and more unplayable. And you don't have to search far as to why their doing this either. It really comes down to them not monetizing the game (they could, though it would involve refactoring a lot of spaghetti code I'd wager); its long-term replayability isn't easily accomplished unlike Battle Roayle; and finally, founders are able to gain V-Bucks through STW. As of right now being a founder is the only way of *earning* v-bucks - i'm sure free v-bucks doesn't please the shareholders. So as usual it can all be pinned down to corporate greed.
