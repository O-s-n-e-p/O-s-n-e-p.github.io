How to neurotypical people go through life feeling? What emotions are they dominated by on a weekly basis. It’s a pointless question to ask though, as even with an answer, what am I to do with it. The topic I want to discuss is about being a minority vs majority, in any aspect of life. Personally, I’ve almost always be in the minority or felt that I must inject myself into the minority. Be it for video game taste, dietary habits, lifestyle, what have you. From a young age I always saw myself as different and unable to fit in because of my deformity, I tried so very hard to fit-in when I was young, but it was all in vain. After giving up these vain attempts I became the exact opposite, I wanted to be the person people told me I was; I became a misfit. It was my identity. This continued and worked well for me throughout elementary and high school. Now that I’ve long left those days behind, I realize that I became a misfit only because I wanted to fulfill peoples’ expectation of me based off of my appearance. So now, me being the recovering people pleaser that I am, I decided to give up this jig and not try to chase any identity, but let myself cultivate my own unique identity. This allows me to feel less pressured and less inadequate. Anyways, now that I’m not deliberately going against the flow anymore, I feel that I can judge things more clearly now, and I’ve become indifferent to a lot of things which I used to needlessly concern myself with.

Sometimes amidst my improving of self I slow down and ponder why I even choose to improve. I fear learning too much; feeling too fulfilled with myself; feeling proud of myself because that all makes me attached to my life. It makes me fear death. When I feel discontent with myself death doesn’t seem so scary. Maybe religion can aid me in this concern. I don’t want to fear death; and I think that’s the greatest mercy anybody can allow themselves. Is it possible for me to be proud of myself and my accomplishments without becoming attached to my existence? Or to pose that question differently, is it possible for me to be on my deathbed, recounting the numerous ways in which I improved myself and not feel disappointed that I must die such an enriched individual? The way I see it, if these accomplishments allowed me to experience a flow-state pleasure, then I shouldn’t feel saddened by my death. However, if it was all simply achieved for aesthetical purposes- to make me a more “cultured/well-rounded” person so-to-speak, or to feed some identity I’m chasing, then I could see how it would be saddening for me to reflect and feel miserable that those achievements served me no purpose in the end. So, I shouldn’t be searching for accomplishment for the sake of enrichment; but rather for the sake of hedonistically pursuing a flow-state. (I am actively entertaining the idea that achieving flow-state regularly is the key to contentment/happiness).
